Why explaining it to a duck actually works

The technique is older than the profession. Say your problem out loud, in order, to something that can't interrupt, can't judge, and definitely doesn't already know the answer. Somewhere around the third sentence, you hear the part that doesn't make sense — because you're finally the one listening to yourself.

Our therapists don't diagnose anything. They don't even understand the question. That's the point. The fix was always yours — they just held still long enough for you to find it.

What a duck brings to the session

  1. Full eye contact, indefinitely
  2. Zero prior assumptions about your code
  3. No urge to jump to a solution
  4. Complete confidentiality (ducks don't talk)

How a session works

1

State the problem

Out loud, slowly, from the very beginning.

2

Explain it again

As if the duck has never seen code before. It hasn't.

3

Notice the snag

The exact line where your own explanation stops making sense.

4

Fix it

Usually mid-sentence, before you even finish explaining.

5

Thank the duck

It won't respond. It rarely does. That's fine.
